# Product boundaries

- Phase 1 is for verified adults (18+) currently enrolled in one of the four approved IIT Madras non-campus BS programme families: Data Science and Applications; Electronic Systems; Aeronautics and Space Technology; Management and Data Science.
- The product is independent. Do not imply IITM ownership, endorsement or official affiliation.
- No minors, other institutions, anonymous roulette, public social feed, open stranger DMs, exact public location or partner surveillance.
- A Like is not consent to message. Private chat requires an active mutual match and no block/restriction.
- Calls, games and virtual dates need explicit invitation and acceptance.
- Relationship Mode needs mutual activation; either partner may leave independently.
- No default call recording. Do not expose private content or verification evidence.
- If a requested task conflicts with these rules, stop and ask for an authorised human decision. Do not solve the conflict by weakening the rules.
