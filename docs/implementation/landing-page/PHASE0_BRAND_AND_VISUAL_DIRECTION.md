## 1. Strategic Creative Reconciliation: *Editorial Romance* × *THE INTERVAL*

> **Epistemic Tag:** *[Approved Brand Constraints vs Design Interpretation]*

### What Must Remain Untouched (Document 10 Approved System)
- **Dark Cinematic Foundation (*Mineral Night*):** `Void #0B0D10`, `Coal #12161B`, `Iron #1A2027`, `Slate #252D37`, `Edge #343D48`. Neutral dark surfaces (`75–88%` share); primary text `Chalk #F0F1EE`, secondary `Silver #B6BDC4`, muted `Ash #8B949E`.
- **Accents Used Sparingly:** `Iris #8580C4` (`2–5%`) for primary CTA / brand signal; `Ember #E18A62` (`0–2%`) for warmth, intimacy, and editorial focus. Never blended into purple-blue gradients or neon glow.
- **Typography (`Instrument Sans`):** Contemporary neo-grotesk throughout (`64–76px` desktop display hero, `42–50px` mobile display hero — **currently under-scaled at `46.5px` / `28.13px`**). Zero decorative serif overhaul, zero monospaced labels except true technical metadata.
- **Geometry & Brand Marks:** `SayMyName` internal camel-casing preserved; Interval Symbol (paired masses separated by deliberate aperture + `Iris` signal) + Grotesk Wordmark (`components/brand/{Logo,Symbol,Wordmark}.tsx`); sharp architectural radii (`0px` frames, `4px` micro, `6–8px` buttons/controls, `10–12px` panels, **zero `999px` pills**).

### How We Reposition Visual Tone to *Editorial Romance* Inside Document 10
Today's `/` obeys the *surface colours* of Document 10 while violating its *human soul* (Document 10 §05: **"Human presence: natural photography, human copy... Emotionally resonant without romantic clichés or synthetic-tech tropes"**).

Repositioning to **Editorial Romance** does **not** mean reverting to ivory paper, serif logos, pink hearts, or couple-cliché stock photos. It means:
1. **Replacing cold engineering schematics** (`THE INTERVAL ARCHITECTURE v1.0`) with **human-scale storytelling** — a dark editorial portrait/atmosphere (commissioned photography or quiet human gesture: warm tungsten/ember light on hands, café note, late-night desk lamp in two cities) paired cleanly with **one sculptural Interval focal artifact** and a **real UI peek of SayMyName (`Discover` card + conversation prompt)**.
2. **Restoring Document 10's full Display Hero typography (`64–72px` desktop, `40–46px` mobile)** so `"Same world. Different cities. A chance to meet."` (or final approved headline) lands with magazine poise rather than SaaS documentation density.
3. **Elevating `Ember #E18A62` subtly in storytelling** (warm key light on campaign scene/sculpture, editorial highlight) while keeping `Iris #8580C4` crystal-clear on action buttons (`Create Account` / `Join SayMyName`).
4. **Honoring Anti-Cliché & Anti-AI-Slop Rules (`Doc 10 §04`):** Strictly no purple-blue aurora meshes, no dot matrices/halftone clouds, no glossy hearts, no robotic hands, no floating glass dashboards, no capsule/pill navbars, and zero Breaking Bad motifs (green periodic tiles, RV, chemistry flasks).

## 3. Three Distinct 3D / Spatial Visual Identity Concepts (`THE INTERVAL`)

All three concepts keep Document 10's `Mass + Cut + Signal` vocabulary (`Void/Coal/Graphite/Matte Ceramic`, single `Ember` warm key light + `Iris` subtle rim/signal, `prefers-reduced-motion` static parity), while expressing **romantic connection across distance** rather than technical software architecture.

### Concept 1: **"Two Lights Across the Interval" (Architectural Warmth & Aperture)**
- **Emotional / Brand Story:** Two solid, dark-stone architectural monoliths face one another across a narrow vertical gap. Inside the gap—never touching prematurely—a single warm `Ember #E18A62` ambient bounce illuminates their opposing inner chamfers, echoing two people in different cities sharing an unexpected moment of recognition.
- **Materials & Lighting:** Matte black basalt/ceramic blocks (`#14181F`), brushed warm-brass inner bevel catching real key light from the right, subtle cool `Iris` horizon rim on the left. No bloom, no floating sparks.
- **How It Works on Landing Page:**
  - *Hero right focal stage (`45%` desktop width)* or softly integrated backdrop behind a **framed Discover story vignette**.
  - Subtle `4°` damped cursor/scroll alignment (optional in progressive enhancement; statically composed by default on mobile/reduced motion).
- **Best Fits:** **Strongest direct bridge between Document 10 brand purity and Editorial Romance.** Zero cliché; timeless even if photography rotates later.

### Concept 2: **"The Folded Letter & Threshold Key" (Tactile Editorial Object)**
- **Emotional / Brand Story:** Inspired by intentional long-distance correspondence—two heavy matte-graphite slate plates resting slightly offset over a crisp, razor-thin `Ember` seal line, like two half-opened envelopes or conversation prompts waiting for both hands to align them.
- **Materials & Lighting:** Layered dark paper-stone texture, anodized aluminium spine (`#252D37`), subtle directional softbox top-light casting real crisp contact shadows on `Coal #12161B`.
- **How It Works on Landing Page:**
  - Can appear as a **hero visual anchor** OR an **interactive "mutual-alignment" transition** into Section 2 (*How SayMyName Works*), visually teaching "Two people say yes before conversation opens" *without* using technical words like "default-deny" or "ACL".
- **Best Fits:** Visitors who need tangible, intimate metaphor (communication, deliberate writing, prompt answers) rather than abstract geometry.

### Concept 3: **"Coarse Geography Glass" (Two Cities, One Shared Meridian)**
- **Emotional / Brand Story:** Two softly curved smoked-glass lenses resting on dark slate, refracting two distinct warm points of light (representing two distant BS cities—say Chennai & Ranchi or Bengaluru & Jaipur, abstractly without GPS maps) whose light paths gently intersect cleanly at center without pinpointing either coordinates.
- **Materials & Lighting:** Smoked optical glass (`transmission`/CSS-raytraced pre-render), matte graphite base plane, subtle warm `Ember` + `Iris` caustic refraction.
- **How It Works on Landing Page:**
  - Visually reinforces *"Same world. Different cities."* AND privacy-by-design (glowing region, never exact pin/street!).
- **Best Fits:** Deepest thematic tie to **"Same world. Different cities. A chance to meet."**; requires careful art direction so smoked glass never reads like cliché SaaS glassmorphism (`Doc 10 §04 R15/R16` warning!).

## 4. Technical Delivery Comparison: How to Render 3D & Photography Within Budget

SayMyName targets Indian non-campus BS students using heterogeneous devices (including mid-range Android phones on 4G/5G and budget laptops). Target performance budgets: **LCP `< 2.5s`**, **INP `< 200ms`**, **CLS `< 0.1`**, and **zero 3D bundle bloat on `/login`, `/onboarding`, `/discover`, `/chats`** (`Document 10 §19`).

| Dimension | Option A: **Pre-rendered High-Res Stills (`AVIF`/`WebP`) + Subtle CSS Transforms / Micro-Video Loop** | Option B: **Custom Lightweight `Three.js` / `@react-three/fiber` (Lazy-Loaded + Static Poster)** | Option C: **Hosted `Spline` Runtime Embed (`@splinetool/react-spline`)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bundle / Network Cost** | **`~35–90 KB`** (single responsive `AVIF`/`WebP` render + `<1 KB` CSS). Zero extra JS runtime! | **`~150–250 KB` gzipped** (`three` + minimal scene + compressed `.glb` `~100–300 KB`), deferred after initial paint via `next/dynamic(..., { ssr: false })`. | **`~500 KB–1.2 MB+`** Spline runtime + external CDN scene JSON/assets (`300 KB–2 MB+`). |
| **Impact on `<2.5s` LCP** | **Best (`<1.3s` realistic):** Hero poster is native `<img>` / Next `<Image priority>`, immediately paintable on first round-trip. | **Good only if static `AVIF` poster renders as LCP** and WebGL hydrates idle/on-desktop only (`>=1024px`). If WebGL blocks LCP -> **Fails `<2.5s` on 4G**. | **High Risk of Failure (`3–6s+` LCP on mobile 4G):** External runtime handshake + large scene download delays visual readiness. |
| **Impact on `<200ms` INP (Mid-Range Android)** | **Virtually zero main-thread cost** (`<5ms` GPU composite opacity/transform). | **Moderate risk if unthrottled:** Shader compilation + frame loop on mobile WebGL can spike INP (`200–500ms`) during scroll/tap. | **High risk on budget Android:** Heavy JS parse + opaque runtime physics/tick loop frequently stalls scroll & CTA clicks. |
| **Impact on `<0.1` CLS** | **Zero shift** with fixed `aspect-ratio` + explicit `width`/`height`. | **Zero shift** if poster and `<canvas>` share exact fixed-aspect wrapper. | Often flickers or pops on scene load unless carefully masked by identical-angle static poster. |
| **`prefers-reduced-motion` & Low-Power / WebGL-Off Fallback** | **Native & effortless:** Pure crisp studio-lit raster composition works everywhere, forever. | Requires manual static fallback image + WebGL context loss handling + IntersectionObserver pause. | Weak control over Spline's internal loop; relies on heavy runtime just to stay still. |
| **Brand Purity & Bespoke Craft** | **Highest raytraced material realism** (Blender/Cycles true smoked glass, matte basalt, real caustics impossible in cheap mobile WebGL!). | Clean for simple matte ceramic monoliths (Concept 1); harder for realistic optical refraction/lighting without large shaders. | Often looks identifiably "Spline pastel plastic" unless heavily custom-lit. |
| **Verdict for SayMyName** | **RECOMMENDED BASELINE FOR V1 (`P0` Recommendation)** | **Optional Progressive Enhancement on Desktop (`>=1024px`) ONLY in Phase 4+ after static baseline proves conversion** | **DO NOT USE (`REJECTED`)** — unacceptable weight, external runtime dependency, and mobile INP/LCP risk |

> **Recommendation:** Build the redesigned landing page cleanly with **Option A (Blender-rendered / high-craft static `AVIF`/`WebP` hero still + crisp UI composition + restrained `180–260ms` CSS Interval alignment transitions)** first—achieving instant visual luxury, raytraced material depth, guaranteed `<2.5s` LCP, and `~0` JS penalty. Only test an optional lazy-loaded `Three.js` desktop enhancement (`Option B`) *after* conversion telemetry shows baseline lift and mobile WebGL budgets pass real device tests.

## 5. Photography Direction & Explicit Visual Anti-Patterns

### Photography Boundaries (Ethical, Authentic & Truthful)
- **Strict Ban (`AGENTS.md` / `Doc 10 §13`):** Never display AI-generated portraits, fake student profiles, stock-smile couples labelled as "Priya, BS Data Science, Pune", or real members' private R2 photos on the public homepage.
- **Safe, Authentic Ways to Show Human Warmth on `/`:**
  1. **Real commissioned atmospheric photography** (clearly styled as *brand campaign imagery*—e.g., an editorial warm-lit coffee cup & notebook at dusk, two silhouettes or quiet candid student moments at an evening station/study desk with natural Indian architectural surroundings, zero fake portrait claims), paired seamlessly with `Void #0B0D10`.
  2. **Clearly labelled illustrative UI preview card** (`"Example profile — Discover view"`, synthetic placeholder art/non-misleading avatar or commissioned model explicitly styled as UI demonstration with **no false verified-member claims**—Wait: confirm legal/brand rule in `Doc 10 L1002-1004`: *"Never present AI-generated portraits, stock portraits or internal demo images as real verified members. When using test fixtures, visibly label the profiles as synthetic demo data"* -> therefore any preview profile card on `/` **must carry a tasteful clear UI badge/caption like `"Product preview · Sample layout"`** and never pass off stock faces as real verified cohort users!).

### Explicit Visual Anti-Patterns Checklist (`Never Ship`)
- ❌ No `"THE INTERVAL ARCHITECTURE v1.0"` wireframe circuit diagrams on the homepage hero
- ❌ No `"Default-deny"`, `"ACL"`, `"Verification Boundary"`, or engineering specs in Hero or Section 2
- ❌ No fake stats (`"10,000+ IITM BS students waiting!"`, fake match counters, fake testimonials)
- ❌ No artificial scarcity (`"Only 12 spots left in Foundation batch!"`), streaks, or unread-ping teasers
- ❌ No glossy pink/3D floating hearts, neon purple-blue gradients, dot grids, or frosted-glass SaaS dashboard tiles (`Doc 10 §04`)
- ❌ No breaking-bad green flasks, periodic tiles, or IIT Madras campus gate photography implying institutional ownership (`D-01` / `Doc 10 §06`)
